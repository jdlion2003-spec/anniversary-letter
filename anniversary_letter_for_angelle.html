My Dearest Angelle,

Happy Two Year Anniversary, My Love

“

Happy two-year anniversary, my love.

It’s crazy to think about how much our lives have changed since year one. Back then, we were still figuring things out, navigating the small fights, learning each other's boundaries, and learning how to love each other well. Now, two years in, everything feels different in the best way possible. We’ve seen each other at our absolute best and our absolute worst, and through it all, we’ve grown so much more mature, so much more in sync, and deeply at peace. I love that we can just be completely ourselves now. No pretense, no masks, just us, seeing each other for who we truly are.

This past year wasn't easy. Moving to Germany to pursue my education and goals was one of the biggest leaps of my life, but it came with heavy moments. There were times at the start when the pitfalls hit hard, and I felt so lost and alone abroad. But you were right there with me, Angelle, feeling those same emotions from across the world.

Even through a screen, across 6,000 miles and a tough time difference, you held me. I swear there were nights when I could literally feel your arms wrapped around me and your head on my shoulder while I cried. I could smell your perfume, feel your warmth, and just breathe again. You felt like the morning sun, strong and intense, yet so gentle and kind.

You became my absolute safe space, guiding me through the isolation and helping me open up in ways I never could before. Even with the distance, I love the small, quiet universe we’ve built through our phones. I love how we can fall asleep on call together, so overwhelmed by each other's presence that we can’t even keep our eyelids open. I love our random singing sessions, and how our humor is just one giant inside joke built on endless memes that only we understand.

And then there are your books. I love watching you completely get lost in them, how you talk to yourself, laugh that weird, wonderful laugh of yours, and react as if you’re living right inside the story. You get so absorbed that you practically forget I exist for a moment, yet somehow you still think of me while reading. Seeing that pure, unfiltered joy makes me want to protect your happiness forever. It’s the exact reason I’ve started caring about your books, too. I just want to experience that world with you, my love.

I know the distance between Germany and Malaysia is hard, and circumstances make it tough to plan every detail of our future. But I’m not scared of the unknown as long as I’m facing it with you. December can’t come fast enough. After a year of being so far apart, finally getting to see you, hold your hand, and just be in the same room is going to make every single lonely night worth it. We might be past the simple, fleeting excitement of the beginning, but what we have now is so much better: the deepest comfort, peace, and joy.

Thank you for experiencing this life with me, for fighting for us, and for being my person. I love you more than words can say, Angelle, and I can’t wait to see what this third year holds for us.

Forever yours, my love,

Josh
